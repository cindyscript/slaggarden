Meaning of Labyrinth, according to Merriam-Webster:

- a place constructed or full of intricate passageways and dead ends. 
- Something extremely complex, or torturous. 


When I think of Labyrinths I think of:
- Dreams
- The pressure to make decisions
- Forbidden knowledge
- Anxiety
- Control
- A journey
- Navigating through a sea of people at an event
- Existential


The origins {paraphrased, from my understanding}:
King Minos commissions Daedalus to construct a maze of confusing pathways, the labyrinth. The labyrinth keeps the minotaur imprisoned in the center. The minotaur was the result of a relationship between Queen Pasiphaë of Crete and the Cretan Bull, a white bull sent by Poseidon for King Minas to sacrifice. According to the myth, Pasiphaë has an unnatural desire for this bull due to being cursed by Poseidon, and thus resulted in the creation of Minotaur.

Minos's son was killed in battle so to appease Crete they demanded a tribute of 7 boys and 7 girls (or 9 in some cases) to be fed to the Minotaur.

Theseus was one of the men who was selected, and Minos's daughter Ariadne gives him a thread so that he can track his way in and out of the labyrinth. Theseus successfully slays the Minotaur.

- Describing/personifying natural events

- Making connections to things that humans cannot fathom or explain
