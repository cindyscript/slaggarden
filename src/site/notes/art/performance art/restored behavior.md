*restored behavior* is a concept developed by richard schechner, an American theater director, author, and academic.

**restored behavior** consists of these key ideas:

- strips of behavior: actions can be cut and rearranged like a strip of film and separated from the original social and psychological element that created them. 
- separation of the self: human actions can be treated as physical objects. an individual can re-embody this suit to work on, practice, and embody it. 
- not for the first time: every action has not been performed the first time. 
- the paradox!: performers are *not* the character in a movie or play. Yet they are also *not* *not* said character because they are merely *embodying and transmitting the character on the stage.*
